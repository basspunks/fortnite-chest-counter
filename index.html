Chest Counter

Starts with Windows, hidden next to the clock, and shows the counter only while Fortnite runs. It hides a minute after the game closes, once the last replay is read. × on the bar only hides it; "Quit completely" (the icon next to the clock) stops it until you open it again.

A Windows notification 5 minutes before a Treasure Hour, and when a replay shows you found a treasure.

During matches the counter looks at the small area right of the crosshair, five times a second, for the "+600 XP / CHEST" text. Nothing is recorded or sent: each look is dropped at once. Needs Windowed Fullscreen.

Only Fortnite's own sound is heard: never Discord, music or anything else on your PC.

Replays

During a match the sound counts live. When it ends, the exact count from Fortnite's replay replaces it. Required: Fortnite → Settings → Game → Replays → Record Replays: On.

Battle Royale on the season's map only. Ranked, Reload, other limited modes and Creative are kept in the list but not in your totals.
